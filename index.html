Get Started

Welcome

Students often lose track of which syllabus topics have been covered in lectures and which ones still need self-study. This website gives you one simple place to organize your course, tick off topics as you finish them, and see your overall progress at a glance.

How It Works

1. Create Your Course

Enter your course name, code, and teacher, then define up to five units with their subtopics.


Create Course

2. Tick Your Topics

Open the units checklist and mark each topic as you complete it. Finished topics are crossed out.


Open Checklist

3. Check Your Progress

See how much of the subject is complete on a 0% to 100% mastery progress bar.


View Progress

Why Use It

Stay Organized

Lecture topics and self-study topics are kept in one clear list.

Prepare Early

Spot the topics you still need to cover well before tests.

Works Everywhere

The layout adapts to laptops, tablets, and phones.
